What we collect, and why

Each item says who can see it.

The name you giveYour group

Your profile photo, if you add oneYour group

Shown next to your name. You can change or remove it in Profile.

Your email address, if you sign inOnly you

You sign in to post ideas and to join or start a group. We use your email to send sign-in codes and to keep your ideas and groups tied to you. Other members don’t see it.

Your Google name and email, if you use “Continue with Google”Only you

We receive the basic profile Google shares (name, email and profile picture link) and use only the name and email, for the same reasons as above. We never see your Google password and don’t access anything else in your Google account.

What you postYour group

So the app remembers your activity on this phone without an account. It’s stored in your browser.

Deleting your information

You can delete any idea you lead from within the app, and its photos go with it.

To delete your account and everything tied to it, email us from the address you sign in with, and we’ll remove it.
