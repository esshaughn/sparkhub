Spark Hub

Privacy

Last updated September 24, 2026

The short version

Spark Hub is a place for groups to post ideas for things to do together and turn them into plans. We keep only what the app needs to work. We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

What we collect, and why

  • The name you give. It’s shown next to ideas you post, lead or offer help on.
  • Your email address, if you sign in. Only people who post ideas need to sign in. We use your email to send sign-in codes and to keep your ideas tied to you. Other members don’t see it.
  • Your Google name and email, if you use “Continue with Google”. We receive the basic profile Google shares (name, email and profile picture link) and use only the name and email, for the same reasons as above. We never see your Google password and don’t access anything else in your Google account.
  • Your name and phone number, if you RSVP. Only the lead of that idea can see them, so they can reach you about the plan.
  • What you post: ideas, locations, dates, photos, offers and interest. These are visible to members of the group.
  • A random ID for your browser, so the app remembers your activity on this phone without an account. It’s stored in your browser.

Who helps us run it

These services process data for us, only to provide the app:

  • Supabase stores the app’s data and photos, and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Resend sends sign-in code emails.
  • Geoapify suggests places as you type a location. What you type in the location box is sent to them to find matches.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.

Deleting your information

You can delete any idea you lead from within the app, and its photos go with it. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email eric@ericscott-creative.com from the address you sign in with, and we’ll remove it.

Questions

Email eric@ericscott-creative.com. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll update the date at the top.
