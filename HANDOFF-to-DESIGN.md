## 3. Behaviour added in the build (no visual change)

- **v8 links:** My groups `#/groups`, My friends `#/friends`, Me `#/me` (it used to open the Profile sheet over Your calendar), My tasks `#/tasks`, All groups `#/explore`; the bare address is still My calendar. A friend link and a friend-request notice open My friends.
- **Friend lists** are kept on the device, per account (v8: in the browser); a list only shows people who are still your friends. *Invite all N to an event…* opens the usual Invite friends sheet with them picked.
- **My tasks' Timeline / Condensed** pick is kept on the device; the chips start on All each visit. The tab's badge still counts events with something to do.
- **Plan an event:** a draft saved on the old Lead step picks up at Review; Real or test is no longer asked or saved (`sparks.test` stays false). The title field takes the focus when the flow opens empty.

- **Event updates** (`20261104000000_event_updates.sql`): *Going and Maybe* is a new audience, `coming` (going or maybe; the old *everyone* also reached people who said Can't). The push's title is the event and its body *Hope: <update>* (the poster's first name; just the update if they have no name). Updates are 200 characters in the app (the table still allows 320, for older ones).
- **Links:** Explore is `#/explore` (`#/calendar` still opens it); Your calendar is the bare address, so a reload, Back with no history, signing out and the old fallbacks (an event that's gone, a group you deleted) land there. *See what's up now →* on the gone card goes to Explore.
- **Torrez Fitness's invite link joins Torrez Fitness only** (owner, 2026-10-03; it used to add Hub on Hunters too). **A new member's Explore starts on the group they joined**; Your calendar and Your tasks start on all groups. Each screen's pick is saved on the device, and a group you've left drops out of it.
- **Soft holds** (`20261103000000_soft_holds.sql`), built as specced in *Soft holds*, with these rules: the poll's first date sets the hold to 7 days on (later dates share it); *Keep holding* and *Hold them again* are the same action, 7 days from today, for the lead and co-leads; a date that has passed drops off; a set date or a cancelled idea ends the holds at once. Polls already running got 7 days from their first date, so most show as lapsed until the lead holds them again. The two reminders go out in the 8am job, never for test or demo events or an idea that's looking for a lead; *Lock it in?* goes once per hold (Keep holding lets it go again), counts the people who voted on any date, and shows in the bell only after the push went.
- **No help needed is saved on the event** (Jobs in the idea's steps): picking it in Start an event marks the idea's *Jobs* step done, like a job would. Adding a job in the flow clears the pick.
- **One shift per person per job** (*Help out jobs*): a shift's *Sign up* takes that shift and drops any other shift you had on the job; *Undo* on the banner moves you back. The old *Pick a shift* sheet (tick several, a note) has no way in any more. The note people left on a shift stays in the database but nothing shows it now (the lead's roster is gone).
- **What to expect's overview** is `sparks.overview` (`20261103030000_what_to_expect.sql`, ≤ 80 characters); the lead and co-leads save it like the details, and group admins through `admin_edit_spark`. It's kept in drafts and copied by *Do it again*.
- **Your calendar's Filter** dropped *Needs help* and *This week*; a group page's Filter still has them.
