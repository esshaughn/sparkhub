Launch help

Help getting your neighborhood on board.

A group only works if neighbors know about it. Groups can get help with their first year: flyers and QR codes, a plan for spreading the word, and someone to call when you're stuck.

What's an active member?

Someone who opened the group, RSVPed, offered to help or signed up for something in the last 30 days. Plans count active members, not everyone who ever joined.

Are the limits enforced now?

No. During the beta the limits are a guide. We're measuring how groups actually use Spark Hub before setting anything.

When will paid plans start, and what will they cost?

Prices will be set before the beta ends. Groups in the beta will hear well ahead of any change.

What if our neighborhood can't pay?

Plans can be sponsored, by a local business or a neighbor who wants to help, so no neighborhood goes without.

Will friends ever pay?

No. Planning with friends is free.
