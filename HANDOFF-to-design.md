## 3. Behaviour added in the build (no visual change)

- **A floated idea is quiet** (owner, 2026-10-02): an idea that needs a lead sends the group no *New idea* push and has no *New idea* row in the bell, whether it was floated in Create event or its lead stepped back. It still shows on the Ideas board with *NEEDS A LEAD*. An ask to lead is the one alert it sends, to the person asked (push and bell, under the *Friends* topic, now described as *Friend requests, friends inviting you to events, and someone asking you to lead*).
- **A tap isn't undone by a late refresh** (2026-10-02): taps that show at once (RSVP, Interested, votes, sign-ups) could flip back for a moment when a refresh that began before the tap arrived after it; tapping again in that moment went the wrong way. Such a refresh is now dropped, and the one after the save lands.
- **Only someone in an idea's groups can take the lead** (2026-10-02): a guest or a link holder from outside the group no longer gets *I'll lead*.
- **Reminders in the bell appear when they're due** (8am the day before, and 8am on the day). Before 8am they used to show stamped *Just now* on every redraw, so *Mark all read* never cleared them.
- **A cancelled idea is read-only** (audit, 2026-10-01): no Sign up, Add, Suggest a date or location, voting, picking or *Make it a plan* (the database already refused them). Plans already worked this way.
- **The *N events with no date yet* strip** (Calendar and Your schedule Month) also switches the sort back to Soonest, so it always lands on *Date TBD* (group pages already did).
- **Text fields are 16px** wherever they were smaller (search, sign-up and job fields, notes, the time picker), so iPhone no longer zooms the page when you tap one.
- **Screen readers** hear *Owner* / *Admin* on the Members sheet's role chips (they said *You're an owner* about other people).
- **Friends are mutual (Update 13):** a request from Members needs a group you share, and Accept makes you friends; a friend link makes you friends as soon as the person opening it says yes. If the person you asked had asked you first, your request just makes you friends.
- **Declining and removing are quiet.** A declined request stays *Requested* for the sender and they can't ask again, unless you send them one yourself (that makes you friends). Removing a friend clears any requests between you, so either can ask again later. Removing an account removes its friendships and requests.
- **Being friends shows nothing extra:** only names and photos (so a link friend in no shared group still has a face). No RSVPs or plans are shared because you're friends.
- **Who can invite friends to an event:** the lead and the group's admins always; anyone going, unless the lead turned *Guests can invite friends* off. Friends only, 30 at a time; anyone already going or already invited is skipped and gets no second notification. An invite lets that person see the event and RSVP (as an event link does), even when it's Private.
- **Friend requests are capped** at 30 an hour per person (*That's a lot of requests at once. Try again in a bit.*).

- **Month view, empty day:** *Start an event on {date}* opens Create event with that date filled in; it's hidden on past days.
- **The TBD strip** switches the Calendar to List (remembered, as picking List would be) and scrolls to *Date TBD*.
- **Make home** happens on Save: new groups are added first, then the home moves (`set_home_group()`), then unticked groups come off.
- **Removing a job** in Edit what you need also sends the note to the people signed up, not just the Help out ✕.
- **Push** follows the in-app feed's rules and each person's topics; a topic that's off is off on the phone too. Demo seeding never pushes, and reminders skip demo events. Signing out removes that phone's push.
- **Home Screen badge** (installed app, notifications allowed): the icon's red number is the bell's unread count; each push adds one while the app is closed, and opening the app sets it back to the real count (cleared at zero).
- **Second address:** https://sparkhub.wereallneighbors.org serves the live app alongside gosparkhub.vercel.app.
- **Admins** who edit a title or Basic details don't get the switch (only the lead posts updates).
- **Feedback inbox unread** (Update 9): a note is *NEW* until the owner closes the inbox on that device (kept in the browser, `spark-hub-feedback-seen`, not the database), so another device shows it as new again. The inbox shows whoever's signed in as a `demo_admins` account (the owner), not a hard-coded email. Feedback keeps its phone notification to the owner.
- **New accounts unread** works the same way (`spark-hub-accounts-seen` on the device). The list comes from `new_accounts()`, which answers only `demo_admins`; "an account" is a confirmed, non-anonymous email, never @example.com (same as the new-account phone notification). *Joined* is when the email was confirmed; the method is Google if the account has a Google sign-in.
- **Send feedback row** in Profile → Settings is gone; the Help & info **Give feedback** tile replaces it. Notification settings stays in Settings → Notifications and the bell's gear.
