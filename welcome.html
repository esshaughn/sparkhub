How it works

From "we should…" to it happened.

1

Float an idea

A street cleanup, a potluck, a Saturday walk. Post it and see who's interested.

2

Someone leads it

A lead picks the date and the place, or puts it to a vote, and makes it a plan.

3

Everyone pitches in

People say they're coming, sign up for jobs, bring something, and share photos after.

For neighborhoods

A home for your neighborhood's plans.

One group for your neighborhood, school or building: a shared calendar, a board of ideas, volunteer sign-ups, and a team that runs it.

  • Ideas anyone can float and back
  • Jobs and shifts people sign up for
  • Reminders on people's phones
  • Neighbors can RSVP without an account

For friends

Your own plans, in the same place.

Keep lists of the friends you plan with, and make private events just for them. They sit on the same calendar as your neighborhood's.

  • Private events for up to 20 people
  • Lists of friends to invite in one tap
  • One calendar for everything you're going to
  • Free

Made for meeting in person

Less scrolling. More showing up.

No feed to scroll. Spark Hub shows what's coming up and what needs a hand, then gets out of the way.
