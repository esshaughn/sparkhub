The beta

Early, free, and still changing.

Spark Hub is being tested with a handful of neighborhoods and friend groups in Austin. Everything is free during the beta, and what you tell us shapes what comes next.

What does beta mean here?

Spark Hub works, and people use it for real plans. It also changes often, and you'll run into rough edges. We fix things quickly when we hear about them.

Is it free?

Yes, all of it, for the whole beta. Groups will hear well ahead of any paid plans. See Pricing.

What happens to my plans and photos?

They stay. Testing doesn't wipe your data, and you can delete your account any time from the app.

How do I tell you something's wrong?

In the app, open Me and tap Give feedback. It goes straight to the person building Spark Hub, who reads every one.
