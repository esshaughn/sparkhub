Spark Hub
How this works

Ideas come to life when we build them together

Spark Hub is where your groups plan things. Anyone can start something, and everyone can help make it happen.

1
Create an event

Add a title and whatever you know. Date, place and details can wait. Let the group vote on them.

2
RSVP & pitch in

Neighbors RSVP, vote on dates and spots, and sign up to bring things or help out.

3
Make it happen

Everyone going gets a reminder the day before. Afterwards, add photos and thank whoever helped.

Good to know

Groups are private. Only members see a group’s events, and you join with a code or a link.

Hosts stay in charge. People can suggest dates and spots. The host decides what the event becomes.

Your tasks keeps track. Anything you’re hosting or signed up for shows up there with what’s left to do.
