The short version

Spark Hub is a place for groups to post ideas for things to do together and turn them into plans. We keep only what the app needs to work.

We don’t sell it
No ads
No tracking cookies

What we collect, and why

Each item says who can see it.

The name you give
Your group

Shown next to ideas you post, lead or offer help on.

Your email address, if you sign in
Only you

Only people who post ideas need to sign in. We use your email to send sign-in codes and to keep your ideas tied to you. Other members don’t see it.

Your Google name and email, if you use “Continue with Google”
Only you

We receive the basic profile Google shares (name, email and profile picture link) and use only the name and email, for the same reasons as above. We never see your Google password and don’t access anything else in your Google account.

Your name and phone number, if you RSVP
That idea’s lead

Only the lead of that idea can see them, so they can reach you about the plan.

Deleting your information

You can delete any idea you lead from within the app, and its photos go with it.

To delete your account and everything tied to it, email us from the address you sign in with, and we’ll remove it.
