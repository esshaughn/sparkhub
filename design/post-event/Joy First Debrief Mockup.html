Joy first, then next time

A past event keeps its full event page, with how it felt moved to the top. Everyone who went gets one card: joy and thanks first. "Next time" opens only after you've shared your joy, and it asks what you could do before what would give you more joy. Tap the notifications to jump to each moment.

The notifications
The rules that keep it joy-first One card, in order. You can't reach "next time" without first tapping what gave you joy. Joy alone counts as a full answer. "I could…" before "more joy from…". Your own part comes first, so it reads as reflection, not homework for the lead. Joy is public. Wishes go to the hosts. The lead gets them grouped and counted on day 2, after the celebration. At most three pushes per person who went, and the day-2 nudge only if they did nothing.
