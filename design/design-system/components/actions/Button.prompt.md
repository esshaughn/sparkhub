Full-pill button in the role colour of the surface it sits on (purple for plans, gold for ideas, green for going).

```jsx
<Button variant="gold" full>Offer to lead</Button>
<Button variant="gradient" full>Make it a Plan!</Button>
```

Text on gold is always #2a1d00, never white. Disabled = grey, still tappable to toast why ("Add a title first").
